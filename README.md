**What Are Old TextNow Accounts?**

Old TextNow accounts are accounts created in the past rather than newly registered accounts. Some third-party sellers advertise these accounts in bulk, but account age alone does not guarantee reliability, continued access, or eligibility for specific features.

Before engaging with a provider, review TextNow's current terms and determine whether the proposed account transfer or use is permitted.

**How to Evaluate 99 Account Providers**

When researching websites that advertise bulk TextNow accounts, consider these factors:

**Provider transparency:** Look for verifiable business information and clear service descriptions.

**Customer support:** Check whether the provider explains its policies and offers a reliable support channel.

**Refund terms:** Understand the conditions, limitations, and dispute-resolution process before paying.

**Privacy protection:** Avoid sharing passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary personal information.

**Policy compliance:** Confirm that the advertised service complies with TextNow's applicable terms and restrictions.

Do not assume that a website is trustworthy simply because it advertises low prices, bulk discounts, or aged accounts.

**Risks of Buying Accounts in Bulk**

Purchasing accounts from unauthorized sellers can introduce several problems, including account recovery disputes, access restrictions, recycled phone numbers, and exposure of personal information. Bulk purchases can multiply these risks when account ownership and account history are unclear.

A seller's claims about account age, number availability, or long-term access should not be treated as guarantees.

**Safer Alternatives for Business Use**

For legitimate communication needs, consider creating accounts through TextNow's official registration process, subject to eligibility and service availability. For software testing, use authorized test environments and documented APIs where available.

Businesses managing customer communications should also compare established business phone services that provide appropriate account controls, support, and transparent terms.
